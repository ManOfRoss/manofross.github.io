Clip Scheme n8n

Privacy policy

OpenWorld Pulse operates Clip Scheme n8n for its YouTube channel. The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos on the channel owner’s instructions.

What the app uses and stores

When the owner grants access through Google, n8n stores the authorization on the owner’s system and uses it to make upload requests. The workflow sends Google the video, title, description and upload settings. Local review records identify the approved file, the upload attempt and, when YouTube confirms success, the video ID. The app uses these records to track uploads and avoid duplicates.

Managing access and copies

The channel owner can revoke the connection in Google Account permissions, manage uploaded copies in YouTube Studio, and request deletion of locally stored records or credentials by emailing OpenWorld Pulse. Revoking access stops future authorized requests. Existing uploads and local records can be removed separately.

See the Google Privacy Policy for Google’s handling of data.
